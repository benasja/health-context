# Profile

Copy this to `profile.md` and edit it. Everything here is optional — write what
is true, delete the rest, leave it short.

**Why this exists:** the export measures what your body did. It cannot record
why. Without that, anything reading your data will fill the gap with a guess,
and the usual guess is that a decline means you lost motivation. Three honest
lines here change every recommendation you get back.

`profile.md` is gitignored. It never leaves your machine.
